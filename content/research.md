## Performance Comparison of ARIMA, Facebook Prophet, and LSTM in Time Series Sales Predictions

Published in 2024, this work compares three important approaches to sales forecasting: **ARIMA**, **Facebook Prophet**, and **LSTM**.

The research reflects my interest in applied data analysis, model comparison, and practical forecasting problems. Rather than treating a model as a black box, the study places different modeling families beside one another to examine how their characteristics matter in a time-series setting.

## Why compare these models?

The three approaches represent different ways of thinking about sequential data:

- **ARIMA** provides a classical statistical framework built around autocorrelation and differencing.
- **Prophet** offers an approachable forecasting workflow designed around trend and seasonality.
- **LSTM** uses recurrent neural-network structures to learn longer-range relationships in sequences.

Comparing them creates a useful bridge between statistical modeling, practical forecasting tools, and deep-learning methods.

## What this work represents

For me, the publication is both an academic milestone and a foundation for deeper postgraduate work. It complements my practical software projects by adding a research perspective: defining a problem carefully, comparing approaches, and communicating results in a structured way.

My current Data Science studies continue this direction through applied modeling, analysis, and stronger technical foundations.

## Building useful systems, one careful decision at a time

I am a Computer Science graduate focused on data, automation, and practical problem-solving. I build software that makes repetitive work faster, clearer, and more reliable—especially where spreadsheets, desktop workflows, and operational safeguards matter.

My recent projects combine **Python, pandas, openpyxl, and modern desktop UI tooling** to solve concrete business problems. They include offline QR generation, complex Excel merging, and backup-safe data-cleaning workflows.

Alongside development, I completed a six-month internship in Cyber Security and IT Support. That experience gave me practical exposure to Microsoft 365, Microsoft Defender, Power Automate, Copilot, and basic networking troubleshooting.

I am currently pursuing an **M.Sc. in CSE with a major in Data Science** at Daffodil International University. My direction is to keep combining careful engineering with applied data work—building tools and analyses that are understandable, dependable, and genuinely useful.

> I care less about adding complexity and more about removing uncertainty from everyday work.

Outside an individual feature, I think about the complete experience: what happens when the input is messy, how someone checks the result, and whether an operation can be reversed safely.
